# Privacy Policy

Revision 1. Effective from 16 November 2026.

## 1. Who is responsible

The operator of the website shelter.mahiron.online and the author of the ShelterSave-Studio program is Lev Burmistrov (Mahiron-hq), Russia. Contact for any question, including data protection: Mahiron.HQ@protonmail.com.

## 2. The desktop program

The ShelterSave-Studio desktop program does not collect, store or send any data about you. It never connects to the network: it has no telemetry, no update checks and no downloads. Links open in your browser only when you click them. Settings, the list of recent files and backup copies of your saves stay on your computer, in your user profile.

## 3. The website: what is processed

**Your save file.** It is decrypted, edited and encrypted only in your browser. Its contents are never sent to the server or anywhere else.

**Visit counting.** When a page is opened, the server briefly uses your IP address and the User-Agent string of your browser to compute a one-way hash with a random salt. The salt exists only in the server's memory and is replaced every day at 00:00 UTC; the previous salt is destroyed. The hashes of the current day are kept only in memory and are erased together with the salt. After the day ends only the number of distinct visitors remains. Your IP address is held in memory only for computing the hash and for limiting the request rate, and is never written to disk or to logs.

The server stores only total counters: visitors and page views per day and hour, views per page, the domain of the referring site (without the full address), device type, browser family and operating system family (without versions), interface language, editor events (files opened and saved, kinds of errors) and clicks on the desktop version download. Each breakdown is stored separately; breakdowns are not combined in one record, so small numbers cannot single out a person. The full statistics are available only to the administrator on the server.

A person who visits on different days is counted once for each day; monthly and total figures are sums of daily figures.

**Server logs** do not contain IP addresses, User-Agent strings or identifiers.

## 4. What is not processed

The website does not process the contents of save files, does not set cookies, has no accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics, widgets or content delivery networks, does not build device fingerprints and does not determine your location.

## 5. Data in your browser

The website keeps your settings and, if you allow it, the unfinished editing session (the open file and the history of edits) in your browser's local storage. A service worker keeps a copy of the website files so the editor can reopen without a connection. This data never leaves your device. You can delete it with the "Clear local data" button in the settings or in your browser settings. Local storage is not used for counting visits.

## 6. Purposes and legal bases

The short use of the IP address and User-Agent serves the operation and protection of the website (request rate limiting) and anonymous visit counting. The legal basis is the legitimate interest of the operator in running a secure website and understanding how much it is used: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and UK GDPR, Article 31 of the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP), and clause 7 of part 1 of Article 6 of Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data". The operator does not sell or share personal information within the meaning of the CCPA/CPRA.

## 7. How to opt out of counting

You are not counted if you switch off "Anonymous visit statistics" in the website settings, or if your browser sends the Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal. Search robots and service checks are not counted either. The editor works the same way whether you are counted or not.

## 8. Retention

IP address and User-Agent: only in memory for the time needed to compute the hash and to limit the request rate. Daily hashes and salt: until 00:00 UTC of the next day. Total counters: without limit; they do not relate to any person.

## 9. Where data is processed and transfers

The server is located in Switzerland. It is rented from a hosting provider that supplies the infrastructure only. No data is transferred to third parties. The administrator receives total figures in Telegram on request; these messages contain only total numbers and no information about visitors.

## 10. Your rights

- **EU and UK (GDPR, UK GDPR):** access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
- **Switzerland (nFADP):** information, correction and deletion, objection, and a complaint to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
- **Russia (152-FZ):** information about the processing, clarification, blocking or destruction of data, withdrawal of consent, and a complaint to Roskomnadzor.
- **California (CCPA/CPRA):** to know, delete and correct personal information, to opt out of its sale or sharing (it is not sold or shared), and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

Send requests to Mahiron.HQ@protonmail.com. The website does not keep data that identifies you, so in most cases there will be nothing to link to your request; you will receive an answer within 30 days in any case.

## 11. Children

The website and the program are not directed at children and do not knowingly collect information about them. Visit counting is anonymous for everyone.

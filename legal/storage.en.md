# Local storage and cookies

Revision 1. Effective from 19 November 2026.

## Cookies

The website **sets no cookies**, neither its own nor third-party ones.

## What is kept in your browser

- **Settings** (language, theme, editor mode, whether you have seen the notice, and others) in local storage (localStorage).
- **The unfinished editing session** (the open file, your edits and the undo history) in IndexedDB, if "Restore unfinished session" is on. This keeps your work safe after a page reload or a browser crash.
- **Website files** (pages, scripts, styles, fonts) in the service worker cache, so the editor opens even without a connection.

This data stays on your device only and is never sent to the server. It is not used to count visits.

## Pictures from the game file

If you load the file of your game to see pictures and wasteland journal phrases, the file is read only in the open tab. Neither the file nor the pictures and phrases are stored in the browser: they are gone when you close the tab.

## How to delete it

- The **"Clear local data"** button in the website settings deletes the settings and the saved session.
- After downloading a file you can delete the session right away: the download window offers it.
- Everything, including the website cache, can be deleted in your browser settings: clear site data for shelter.mahiron.online.
